=== Section 5: text-composites ===

Reading comprehension — Seasons
Article: Snow is white. Grass is green.
1. True or false: Snow is white. Answer: True.

Word bank — Complete the sentence
Bank: A. white B. green. Do not reuse words.
Snow is [1]. Grass is [2]. Answers: 1 A; 2 B.

Cloze — Complete the statement
Snow is [1]. 1: A. white B. green. Answer: A.

Ordering — Sort ascending
Items: 0. Two; 1. One. Answer order: 1, 0.

Matching — Match words and numbers (one-to-one)
Left: 0. One; 1. Two. Right: 0. 1; 1. 2.
Answer: left 0 -> right 0; left 1 -> right 1.
